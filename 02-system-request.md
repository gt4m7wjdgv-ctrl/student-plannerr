# 02 - System Request

## 1. Project Sponsor
The project sponsor is a student who needs a tool to organize academic activities.

## 2. Business Need
Students may have difficulty managing class schedules, study plans, assignments, projects, and exam dates when these activities are recorded in different places. The proposed system provides a single place to organize and track these responsibilities.

## 3. Business Requirements

The system should:

- Help students organize their weekly class timetable.
- Allow students to create daily and weekly study plans.
- Allow students to record assignments and projects with due dates.
- Allow students to record exam dates.
- Show pending and completed tasks.
- Display upcoming academic activities.

## 4. Business Value

The system is expected to reduce forgotten deadlines, help students organize their time, make academic tasks easier to track, and provide a clear view of upcoming responsibilities.

## 5. Special Issues and Constraints

- The first version is intended for individual student use.
- The system will not include online payment.
- The system will have a simple and user-friendly interface.
- Proposed technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and SQLite.
